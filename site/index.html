Sorted by what it’s for照用途自動分好

Every message is labelled on your Mac: work, people, transactions, notifications, dev, newsletters, promotions. Pick a topic to see only that kind of mail. Reorder, hide or add your own in Settings.每封信都在你的 Mac 上自動標好用途：工作、關係、交易、通知、開發、電子報、促銷。點一個類別就只看那一類；在設定裡可以排序、隱藏，或加上自己的類別。

AI that only proposesAI 只提議，不動手

Press ⌘J to ask your own Claude Code or Codex about your mail. It searches and reads through MailSift, gives a reason for every message it wants to change, and can start a reply draft. Nothing changes until you confirm, and past conversations stay in the history.按 ⌘J，用你自己的 Claude Code 或 Codex 問信件。它透過 MailSift 搜尋、讀信，想改動的每封信都附上理由，也能幫你起草回覆。你按確認之前什麼都不會動，聊過的對話也會留在歷史裡。
